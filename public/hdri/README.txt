Sky photographs for The Glass Residence (equirectangular Radiance .hdr, 2:1, 4k recommended):
  day.hdr     bright blue sky, soft clouds, garden / tree line at the horizon
  sunset.hdr  warm golden-hour sky with a low sun
  night.hdr   deep blue dusk or night sky
Missing files are fine: that time of day falls back to the procedural sky and the console shows a warning.
The sun light is turned to match each photograph's sun. Tune brightness in src/world/look.js (modes.*.sky).
