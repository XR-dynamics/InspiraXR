Drop your real panorama here as  panorama.jpg  (equirectangular, 2:1, 4096x2048 or 8192x4096).
When the file exists the app uses it instead of the generated mountains and sky. The sea is still drawn in front of it.
Optional: panorama.json  {"yaw": 0, "horizon": 0.0}  rotates the photo (degrees) and lifts/lowers it (-1..1).

Current panorama.jpg: 4096x2048 equirectangular photograph of Acadia (Maine), taken from the Pannellum project's
example files (github.com/mpetroff/pannellum, examples/examplepano.jpg). It is used unedited. Check the licence of
that file before publishing the app commercially, or replace it with your own panorama.
